Thesis

Physical. Workflow. Data. Distribution.

If a Kingmaker company cannot name those four, it is not a Kingmaker company. The last decade paid people to wrap other people's models. This decade pays people who own the job on the ground.

What the three companies actually own.

Gridhall owns the path from a tradesperson to a live hall. The data is the candidate, the credential, the ticket. The distribution is the plants that will hire. The workflow is diagnose, fund, ticket. The physical plant is the data center itself.

Ganttry owns the clocks on a construction pad. The data is factory slots, cure strength, crane windows. The distribution is developers and EPCs who already bleed demurrage. The workflow is the three clocks. The physical plant is the pad.

Verdict owns the docket that says yes or no to a site. The data is ratepayer, water, town, grid, title. The distribution is the IC and the municipal desk that have to live with the answer. The workflow is Green-Light versus red flag. The physical plant is the land and the interconnect.

None of the three is a chatbot. None of the three is a token. None of the three spends a year in a cave. The first customer conversation is part of the build, not a sequel.

What would kill a company here.

A generic assistant. A wrapper on a frontier model with no unique docket. A token because the press release needed the letters A and I. A public-market ticker purchased for the same reason. A year of product before a single buyer is named. A plan to out-model OpenAI.

Those are not sins of taste. They are a cap table with no distribution, no data, no workflow, and no plant. Kingmaker will not fund that, including from the Chairman's own restlessness.
