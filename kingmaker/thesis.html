Thesis

Where intelligence becomes physical.

Do not ask which industries AI will disrupt. Ask where intelligence gets cheaper, faster, more autonomous, and then gets a body. That map is how Kingmaker picks the next chair. The four filters still gate every company: distribution, proprietary data, workflow, physical plant.

The stack, not ten industries.

Intelligence to compute to energy to robots to biology to autonomy to space. Healthcare sits on that stack. Defense sits on it. A wrapper on a chatbot does not.

  1. 1 year Agents eat tasks. Own picks and shovels, plus anyone with data and a channel. Cybersecurity becomes mandatory the moment an agent can act.
  2. 3 years AI becomes a workforce. One human manages ten agents. AI-native companies with a tenth of the headcount make traditional shops look expensive.
  3. 5 years Physical AI. Humanoids, halls, warehouses, hospitals, homes. Software replaced computer work. A robot attacks the labor economy.
  4. 10 years AI designs biology. Help the doctor becomes help design the treatment becomes design the molecule. Drug discovery stops being only a software story.
  5. 15 years Autonomous physical economy. Construction, manufacturing, freight, farms, defense. Companies that own the machines, not just the model.
  6. 30 years Intelligence as infrastructure. Energy is the constraint. Biology is programmable. Space is economic because the crew is not human.

McKinsey has put healthcare gen-AI opportunity in the hundreds of billions and broader healthcare improvement near a trillion. Data-center electricity is the physical tell: consumption is forecast to roughly double this decade. Those are other firms' numbers. We use them as weather, not as a pitch deck TAM. Global venture at six hundred billion is the wrong TAM. Our TAM is the companies we can birth and staff.

The no list is still the strategy.

  • We will not start another generic chatbot.
  • We will not build an AI wrapper with no distribution.
  • We will not chase every new AI token.
  • We will not buy a ticker because the press release said AI.
  • We will not spend a year in a cave before a customer is named.
  • We will not compete with OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google.
