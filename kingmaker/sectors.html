Lab lanes

Liquid cooling and thermal

Racks at thirty to sixty kilowatts make air a toy. The king is the mechanical lead who has actually piped a CDU. The studio would bring simulation, vendor access, and a hyperscale door. Not a company until a pilot is paid.

Microgrid and energy arbitrage

Power is the bottleneck. Independent producers sit on land and generation and sell it like it is 1998. The king is the IPP. The studio would bring the trading engine and a hyperscaler offtake. Margin on the uplift, not a slide about fusion.

Creator distribution

Agency incubators win because CAC is zero and the roster is the lab. UTA, CAA, Night, Gushcloud. Kingmaker is not those firms. CreatorFi is the letterhead for creator credit and influencer origination. If a consumer spin needs an audience, we borrow that method. We do not pretend the studio is a talent agency.

Maritime and freight

Ninety percent of trade moves by sea. Carbon rules, congestion, and war still live on spreadsheets. The king is the chief mate and the independent broker. The studio absorbs compliance and takes a cut of fuel and time saved.

UGC and interactive worlds

AAA is breaking under two-hundred-million budgets. Modders ship worlds in weeks. The bottleneck is IP clearance and a server, not a concept artist. Cool. Not a Kingmaker chair until distribution is unfair and the data is not public art.

Adaptive learning

Clinicians who already hold the framework. School-district procurement is the wall. Worthy only with FERPA and a district that will pay. Otherwise it is another app.

Professional services, after the pyramid collapses

Entry-level finance, legal, and consulting are being eaten. Hours are a dying unit. Kingmakers in those trades do not sell more associates. They sell outcomes. Boutique partners get the output of a two-hundred person firm, with insurance and a stack they did not have to build. We will not chase a generic legal chatbot. We would elevate a partner who already has the clients and the liability, then give them agents with a backstop.

Same pattern for a single dealmaker running a middle-market book, a tax architect, a field-service roll-up. The king has the relationships. The studio has the stack. Headcount stops being the product.
