Nomenclature

Studio. Not incubator. Not lab.

The words get used as synonyms. They are not. Kingmaker picked one on purpose. Venture studio. The studio is the co-founder. The operator is hired. The idea does not walk in off the street.

Three models. One of them is us.

Venture studio Incubator Idea lab
Goal Build and launch companies from inside. Nurture external founders. Explore and prototype.
Where ideas come from The studio and the Chairman's book. Applicants with a deck. Corporate R&D or a university brief.
Founding team Studio is co-founder. A CEO is recruited to run the spin. External founders keep the wheel. Internal teams. Rarely an independent company.
Capital and equity Seed in kind and in cash. Thirty to eighty percent until the chair is filled. Small checks. Founders keep control. Budget, not a cap table.
What the team actually does Research, product, first customers, hiring. Advice, space, credits. Feasibility, user research, a shelf.
Where it ends An independent company with an operator. A graduate, still theirs. Back into the parent, or nowhere.

Kingmaker sits in the first column. Gridhall, Ganttry, and Verdict were not applications. They were written here. The empty chair on each is the point. When a human takes that chair, the company is real. Until then Gary is Founder and the studio is the factory.

Why this, for this Chairman.

He does not want twenty-five public founder badges. He wants companies that throw off mailbox money and employ people. A studio lets him originate the thesis, keep the equity, and still find a human to run the days. An incubator would make him a landlord of other people's slides. A lab would make him a researcher. Neither matches the work.

Red Rock remains the co-invest fund. Checks into other people. SpaceX, Brick AI, RoboTech. That is a different URL. Kingmaker does not buy those. Kingmaker births these.
