Lab lane · not in the book yet

Rebuild the function. Do not sell a tool.

Healthcare is the most defensible playground for a studio that knows how to wait. The model is not the bottleneck. HIPAA, FDA, liability, a twelve-to-eighteen month hospital sale, and a physician who will put their name on it. That is kingmaking. This page is a map. It is not a company until it passes the engine.

Why this lane exists

McKinsey has estimated generative AI in the tens of billions annually inside pharma and medical products, and broader healthcare improvement on the order of a trillion. Their 2025 survey work put roughly half of U.S. healthcare organizations already on gen-AI, with more than half chasing agentic proofs. Venture has followed: thousands of AI-enabled healthcare companies launched in a decade, funding still selective and concentrating. Weather, not our TAM.

The interesting move is not "we sell an AI tool to hospitals." It is "we rebuilt a healthcare function around AI." Insurance. Primary care. Diagnostics. Trials. A pharmacy. Eventually a hospital. That is a full-stack operator, which is also how these plays die: they take clinical risk and state boards and then drown in overhead. Kingmaker will not romanticize that. We will not fund an overlay on Epic and call it generational.

Four chairs we would actually consider

Super-practice engine

Private groups drown in prior-auth, coding, denials. Elevate a CMO who already runs the clinic. Agents on appeals and ambient notes, bundled with a malpractice rider. Yield is a cut of unlocked revenue, or equity in the group. Not a chatbot for a waiting room.

Trial matching

Most trials slip on recruitment. Elevate the PI. Scan de-identified pools, draft IRB, sell the cohort to a sponsor at six figures. Clock is days of delay, which pharma already prices.

Diagnostics and RPM

Specialists sit in academic centers. Rural systems do not. Elevate the fellowship director who owns the labeled set. Bill on CPT. Share the scan.

Post-acute orchestration

Readmission penalties. Nurse turnover. Voice agents on daily check-in, escalation to a human. Shared savings, not a seat license.

Where the model breaks

Epic and the incumbents compress copilots and hospital automation. Full-stack providers take liability and then the cost of care. Biotech is a trial business, not ARR. Data is the moat, and open models do not have longitudinal genomics plus pathology plus outcomes. Screen any healthcare chair on four things: do they own the outcome or a subscription. Do they own a closed loop. Are they in the record or floating on a web view. Do they have a clearance or a Phase signal, not a waitlist.

Names in the wild are weather: Tempus, Abridge, Hippocratic, Formation Bio, Isomorphic, Recursion, NVIDIA BioNeMo. Kingmaker does not claim them. Red Rock might write a check. The studio births something with an operator, or it births nothing.
