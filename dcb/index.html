Founder & Managing Partner

Gary LaDrido

Gary LaDrido is an American entrepreneur, investor, board member, and sports-team owner with a track record of capital deployment spanning sports, renewable energy, technology, and entertainment. He founded DCB Sports to invest in the fastest-growing alternative asset class in the world.

Through DCB Sports, Gary has built a portfolio that spans golf, with The Bay Golf Club (TGL), Good Good Golf, and TMRW Sports; esports, with Guild Esports; and football, with Venezia FC, AS Monaco Féminin, and Zealots United, a DCB portfolio company. That portfolio also includes BIG3 Basketball and the National Thoroughbred League.

Prior to his sports career, Gary spent two decades in impact investment banking, specializing in energy transition and climate technology. During that period he co-founded Renewable Advisors and Sustainable Capital Markets, and he previously served on the Morgan Stanley Clean Energy Team.

Beyond sports, he is Founder of Red Rock Ventures (a specialist investor in frontier markets) and sits on the Advisory Board of Fenix Space. He recently joined the Board of Directors of the Los Angeles Sports Council ahead of the LA28 Olympics.

He is also Co-Founder and Vice Chairman of Elysium Partners, which invests in entertainment, media, and lifestyle. Its focus spans music, film, fashion, and celebrity NIL (name, image, and likeness) rights, as well as high-value, IP-heavy, brand-driven sectors.
