The firm

An independent advisor built for sustainable capital markets.

Renewable Advisors provides strategic counsel to clean technology, renewable energy, and sustainable infrastructure clients. The craft is M&A, IPO, capital raising, and private placement. The buyer the energy transition was waiting for is now the data center.

What we do

Five pillars. One partner.

From advisory and placement to co-investment and the power behind AI.

Pillar 01

Energy Transition

Impact investment banking and strategic advisory for clean technology and renewable energy. M&A, capital raising, and private placements of equity and debt.

Pillar 02

GP Advisory

Placement-agent services for general partners raising third-party capital. Narrative, data room, and LP syndication across institutional and family-office networks.

Pillar 03

Sustainable Infrastructure

Project finance and structuring for utilities, IPPs, developers, and lenders across solar, wind, storage, grid, and electrified transport.

Pillar 04

Co-Invest

Dedicated SPVs investing 1 to 10 percent pari passu alongside best-in-class GPs, giving family offices direct, fee-efficient access to individual deals.

Pillar 05

AI Power Infrastructure

Advising and financing the power behind data centers. Structuring capital for developers and projects, and raising SPVs to co-invest alongside them.
