FAQ

Real-world asset tokenization, explained

What is tokenization of real-world assets?

Tokenization of real-world assets (RWA) converts ownership or the cash flows of a tangible or income-producing asset into regulated digital securities that can be owned fractionally, distributed globally and potentially traded on a secondary market.

What real-world assets can be tokenized?

Rialto Elysium tokenizes music catalogs, film and TV rights, fashion and consumer-brand IP, sports, gaming, renewable energy, leisure & recreation, digital infrastructure (AI-adjacent power and data centers), and strategic commodities such as gold, lithium, copper and rare-earth elements.

Is real-world asset tokenization regulated?

Yes. Offerings are structured within U.S. securities regulation on a FINRA-member broker-dealer with an SEC-registered transfer agent and alternative trading system.

How do I tokenize my asset with Rialto Elysium?

Share your asset with us; we underwrite and structure it into a bankruptcy-remote vehicle, then raise capital via Reg A+ and Reg D. You receive non-dilutive capital while retaining ownership and control.

Can you tokenize data centers & digital infrastructure?

Yes. We tokenize data centers and AI-adjacent digital infrastructure, including the power assets and facilities behind the AI economy, into regulated digital securities. We do the same across music, film, sports, brands and strategic commodities.
