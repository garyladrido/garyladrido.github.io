The problem

Creators are asset-rich, but liquidity-poor

Especially in gaming, income is lumpy and concentrated, roughly 72% of Twitch streamers earn nothing, while brands and platforms pay the rest 30–90 days late. Banks don't recognize audiences or brand deals as collateral. Creators can't invest in growth at the exact moment it compounds.

⏱

Volatile income

Earnings swing with algorithms, ad rates and project timing, impossible to plan or hire against.

🏦

No credit access

Traditional lenders don't underwrite brand deals, royalties, or audience IP. Creators are invisible to banks.

🧾

Cash trapped in receivables

Money already earned sits unpaid for months, right when creators need it to grow.

What we do

The bank for creators

Two products land creators fast and grow with them, with a WealthTech platform coming downstream.

PRODUCT 1 · THE WEDGE

Factoring: instant liquidity

We advance creators cash today against completed brand campaigns and platform royalties, then collect from the brand or platform later. Fast, low-risk, and the front door to every relationship. ~80% of volume comes from major brands and agencies.

PRODUCT 2 · THE ENGINE

Creator Partnership Program

For premium creators: growth capital in exchange for the exclusive right to manage and commercialize their content for 2–4 years, the music-label model, applied to creators. Capped at 30% LTV, principal back within ~12 months.
